I never wanted the person I love to feel like she isn't good enough because of my struggles. I want us to rebuild, slowly… gently… together. 🫂❤️

Not by forgetting the pain.

But by understanding each other better. By learning from what happened. By becoming more patient with each other. By giving each other the reassurance, space, love, and understanding that we both need.

My love for you isn't something I want to throw away because things became difficult. ❤️

Maybe we can build something even better.

Something healthier.

Something stronger.

Something where both of us can breathe.

Something where both of us feel loved.

Something where we don't have to be perfect, because we know that at the end of the day, we're still choosing to understand each other. 🥹❤️‍🩹

And honestly, right now… I wish I could just be next to you.

I wish I could hug you so tightly and just stay there for a while. 🫂🥺❤️

No arguments.

No questions.

No overthinking.

Just me holding you and reminding you that I'm sorry.

Maybe I'd look at you with tears in my eyes and tell you, "I'm sorry, Maa. Thank you for loving me even when I was difficult to understand. Thank you for reassuring me even when you were tired yourself. I'm sorry I made you feel like you weren't enough when you were always enough." 😭❤️

I'm willing to change.

For everything I did that made your heart tired.

For every moment I made you feel overwhelmed.

For every time my insecurities became your burden.

I hope we don't just go back to what we were.

I hope we rebuild.

Hand in hand. 🫂

Heart to heart. ❤️

With a little more patience.

A little more understanding.

A little more forgiveness.

And a whole lot more love. 🥹❤️‍🩹
